# AI Red Team

Nubesti runs authorized, repeatable security tests against assets you designate. Agents can replay playbooks on a schedule you choose. Findings are meant to include evidence a reviewer can accept or reject.

## What we will say

- Tests start only after paid KYC and a written authorization for the scope.
- Workflows can be mapped to MITRE ATT&CK techniques and OWASP risk classes. Mapping is not a claim that every technique ran.
- Reports should show which mapped checks ran, with request/response evidence where the playbook captured it.
- Draft remediations, when offered, are a starting point — not an automatic merge.
- You can stop in-progress jobs from the portal.

## What we will not invent

- No public 99% accuracy figure
- No “full MITRE ATT&CK coverage”
- No current SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / PCI / HIPAA certification of Nubesti
- No invented data-center count, concurrent-scan capacity, or customer logos
