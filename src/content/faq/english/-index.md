      - active: true
        title: "What does Nubesti do?"
        content: |
          Nubesti runs continuous autonomous security tests against assets you authorize. Findings include technical evidence so a reviewer can accept or reject them. Tests do not start until paid KYC is complete.
      - active: false
        title: "How is this different from a quarterly pentest?"
        content: |
          A traditional pentest is a point-in-time engagement. Nubesti is built to replay the same authorized scope on a schedule you choose. It does not replace every form of manual assessment, and it does not claim to find every vulnerability.
      - active: false
        title: "Do you cover all of MITRE ATT&CK or OWASP Top 10?"
        content: |
          Workflows can be mapped to MITRE ATT&CK techniques and to OWASP risk classes. Mapping is not the same as executing every technique in the matrix. Reports should show which mapped checks ran.
      - active: false
        title: "What accuracy do you claim?"
        content: |
          We do not publish a 99% accuracy figure. Findings are validated with exploitability checks and evidence to reduce scanner noise. False positives can still occur.
      - active: false
        title: "Is Nubesti SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?"
        content: |
          No. We do not display those logos as current certifications. Platform controls are described in the [Trust Center](/trust/) and [legal security overview](/legal/security/). You can use dated findings in *your* audit packs.
      - active: false
        title: "Is it safe to test production?"
        content: |
          You choose the scope. Many teams start on staging and promote a thin production scope. Rate limits and a portal stop control are part of the intended safety model. See [Rules of Engagement](/legal/engagement/).
      - active: false
        title: "How do I start?"
        content: |
          Create an account in the [portal](https://app.nubesti.com), complete paid KYC, designate assets you own or have written permission to test, then launch. Sales walkthroughs: the [contact](/contact/) page.
